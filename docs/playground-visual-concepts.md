# Playground Visual Concepts

Bead: `bw-0dw.10`

This design gate now has three reviewable concepts implemented on the
playground first screen at `apps/playground/app/page.tsx`. Each direction shows
light mode, dark mode, a desktop shell, and a compact mobile preview with the
same mocked L402 challenge, protocol-field panel, and paid Pokedex demo area.

## Concept A: Ledger Desk

- Quiet analyst surface for inspecting credentials and payment state.
- Balanced panel density and low visual noise.
- Best fit for docs-forward demos and careful debugging sessions.

## Concept B: Signal Console

- Higher-contrast operations console with denser telemetry.
- Stronger cypherpunk posture without copying legacy AGPL UI.
- Best fit for live proxy monitoring and fast payment-flow scanning.

## Concept C: Market Terminal

- Compact commercial workbench for comparing prices, invoices, and access.
- More transactional and tab-oriented than the other directions.
- Best fit for repeated middleware and backend-adapter testing.

## Owner Gate

Acceptance cannot close until the owner selects one direction or requests a
revision. Once selected, record the choice here or in the bead notes so
`bw-0dw.1` can apply the approved design system.
